Explore the instruments that measure gravity — and the emerging science of using its patterns when satellite navigation cannot be relied upon.

Earth's gravity is not a radio signal. Gravity-aided navigation still depends on reference maps, sensors, inertial systems and processing; it is not a universal replacement for GNSS.

Classification, not endorsement.

Gravimeters measure gravity; gravity gradiometers measure spatial gradients. These categories are not interchangeable. No listed instrument is claimed to deliver satellite-free navigation on its own. All information should be checked with the manufacturer before procurement.

NAVIGATION EVIDENCE / EDITION 001

Gravity does not
broadcast a position.

Its spatial variations can help constrain navigation when measured against reference maps and integrated with inertial systems. We summarize public evidence, not an independent engineering test.

01

Measure

Onboard sensors collect gravity observations.

02

Match

Processing relates measurements to reference gravity maps.

03

Constrain

The result can help constrain a navigation solution, subject to noise, map quality and conditions.
