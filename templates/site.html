NAVIGATION EVIDENCE / EDITION 001

Gravity does not
broadcast a position.

Its spatial variations can help constrain navigation when measured against reference maps and integrated with inertial systems. We summarize public evidence, not an independent engineering test.

01

Measure

Onboard sensors collect gravity observations.

02

Match

Processing relates measurements to reference gravity maps.

03

Constrain

The result can help constrain a navigation solution, subject to noise, map quality and conditions.

A third-party escrow process can be proposed if both sides agree and the provider accepts the transaction. No active escrow account, partnership, transaction or guaranteed outcome is represented here.

    Private inquiry

    Interested party identifies the intended scope and a channel for confidential discussion.

  2. 02

    Scope & verification

    Both sides agree in writing on the domain, any eligible ancillary assets, price, fees, inspection period and applicable conditions.

  3. 03

    Independent escrow, if agreed

    A separately verified escrow provider may hold buyer funds before the registrar transfer begins, in accordance with its rules.

  4. 04

    Transfer & inspection

    Buyer confirms registrar control and checks the agreed deliverables within the specified inspection arrangements.

  5. 05

    Settlement

    Funds are released under the provider's rules and the transaction agreement; neither party relies solely on an email promise.
