# Public editorial and sourcing policy

Gravimeters.com is an independent reference project. Entries link to their primary sources and are descriptive rather than tested product reviews.

- We distinguish **gravimeters** (gravity measurements) from **gradiometers** (gravity gradient measurements). Multi-function devices are labelled accordingly.
- "Relative", "absolute", "quantum", and "portable" describe different axes; they are not mutually exclusive product classes.
- An instrument is not certified, compared, rated, recommended or independently tested by being included in this atlas.
- We do not publish prices, customer relationships or device specifications unless traceable to a dated public source.
- Navigation depends on sensors, mapping, inertial systems, calibration, algorithms and scenario constraints. Passive gravity is not a promise of standalone positioning.
- Public articles are not original experimental research. Research preprints and corporate announcements are identified by source type.
- To request a correction, contact agent@sohadot.com with a source and the page concerned.
- Acquisition and strategic partnership inquiries are handled separately from independent editorial coverage; no paid placement changes evidentiary conclusions.
